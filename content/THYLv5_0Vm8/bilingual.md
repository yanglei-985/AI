[00:44] The Kelly criterion calculation is used to calculate the optimum stake or position size for a given event, and is made up of four factors. The equity balance, the expected return from a winning outcome, the probability of winning, and the probability of losing. Ultimately, the Kelly criterion formula calculates the perceived edge you have over an event and varies the stake accordingly, thereby determining the optimum stake size, or in trading terms, determining the optimum position size.
> 凯利公式用来计算某个事件的最优下注额（仓位大小），由四个因素构成：资金余额、获胜时的预期回报、获胜概率、失败概率。它本质上是根据你认为自己比市场「多出的优势（edge）」来调整下注额。

[01:16] Contrary to the trading community, position size should not be a predetermined input to a strategy, but rather an output depending on each of these four variables.
